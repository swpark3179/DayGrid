DayGrid is a free personal calendar and to-do app for Korean-speaking iPhone users. The UI is Korean only; English for labels is given in parentheses.

1. Screen recording
- Attached in App Review Information > Attachment. Recorded on [[iPhone model]] with iOS [[iOS version]], starting from launching the app on the Home Screen.
- The app has no accounts (no registration, login or account deletion), no content shared with other users (notes are private to the user), and no paid content, in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so none of these appear in the recording.

2. Purpose and target audience
- For individuals in Korea who want events and to-dos on one monthly grid, together with Korean public holidays and birthdays.
- Problem: typical calendar apps keep undated to-dos elsewhere and handle Korean substitute holidays, election days and Korean lunar birthdays poorly.
- Value: events, tasks, holidays and solar/lunar birthdays on the month grid; undated tasks wait in a Later list and are dragged onto a date; recurring tasks are tracked. No account, no ads; data stays on the device and in the user's own iCloud.

3. How to use the main features
No setup, login or sample files are needed. The app starts with an empty calendar.
- Tabs: 달력 (Calendar), 나중에 (Later), 반복 (Recurring), 설정 (Settings).
- Calendar: tap + to add an event. Long-press a date and drag across days to create a multi-day event; release without moving for a one-day event. Tap a date to open the day sheet.
- Day sheet: long-press an item and drag it onto another date to move it (drop on the X at the top to cancel). Swipe a row to duplicate, move to Later or delete; 실행 취소 (Undo) reverts it.
- Event editor: title, date and time, category, Markdown note (본문) with preview, reminders.
- Later: type a task and press return. Long-press a task and drag it onto a date in the calendar overlay to schedule it.
- Recurring: tap + to add a daily, weekly or monthly task, then check off the current cycle.
- Settings > 생일 (Birthdays): add a solar or lunar birthday, or tap 연락처에서 가져오기 (Import from Contacts).
- Settings > 동기화 (Sync) shows the iCloud sync status.
- Lock Screen widget 남은 반복 할일 shows recurring tasks due today.
- Permissions: Contacts is asked only when importing birthdays; Notifications only when a reminder is turned on. All notifications are local.

4. External services
- Apple iCloud (CloudKit private database, container iCloud.io.github.swpark3179.DayGrid): optional sync using the Apple Account signed in on the device. Without iCloud the app works fully and keeps data on the device.
- GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com): one public JSON file of Korean election days and temporary holidays, checked at most once a day. No user data is sent. A copy is bundled, so the app also works offline.
- GitHub Pages: support and privacy policy pages, opened in Safari.
- Open-source code bundled in the app and run on the device: markdown-it (MIT) and DOMPurify (Apache-2.0) for the note preview. The Korean lunar calendar table is built in (KASI data via the MIT-licensed korean_lunar_calendar package).
- No authentication, payment, AI, analytics, advertising or tracking services, and no third-party SDKs.

5. Regional differences
The app functions the same in all regions. The UI is Korean, and it always shows South Korean public holidays. No feature changes by region or storefront.

6. Regulated industry or third-party material
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party material. Holiday rules come from public Korean government regulations, and the open-source license files are bundled with the app.
